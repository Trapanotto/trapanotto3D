# Texture generation

Both textures were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this project.

## `public/textures/weathered-steel.png`

> Use case: game texture. Asset type: production-ready seamless square tileable albedo texture for a realistic first-person shooter. Orthographic front-on macro photograph of old dark blue-gray painted industrial steel, with subtle scratches, faint oxidized orange corrosion, chipped paint, grime in irregular patches and realistic micro detail. Uniform diffuse studio lighting, no highlights or shadows baked in. Physically plausible subdued midtones. No seams, no raised panels, no bolts, no writing, no icons, no logos, no perspective, no borders. Texture must tile seamlessly on all four edges.

## `public/textures/industrial-concrete.png`

> Use case: game texture. Asset type: production-ready seamless square tileable albedo texture for a realistic industrial first-person shooter. Orthographic overhead close-up of aged dark concrete workshop floor: natural fine aggregate, oil stains, dust, scratches and faint hairline cracks. Neutral charcoal gray with subtle warm brown traces, very detailed but visually restrained. Flat even diffuse lighting, no hard shadows or perspective. No seams, no writing, no symbols, no objects, no borders. Texture must tile seamlessly on all four edges.
